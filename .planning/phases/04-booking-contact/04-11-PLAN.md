<objective>
Close code-review warning WR-01: a single lost slot-taken race currently marks an entire
date as fully booked in the month calendar.

Purpose: `SlotList.handleSlotTakenRefetch()` calls `onFullyBookedDate(dateKey)`
unconditionally, and `BookingCalendar` line 125 adds that date to `fullyBookedDates`, whose
`disabled` matcher then renders the whole date unselectable. A weekday with 6 slots that
loses a race on 1 of them still has 5 genuinely free slots, yet the customer is pushed to
a different day entirely — until a month change triggers a real `refreshMonthAvailability`.
Day-level state must be derived from the day-level refetch, never inferred from one
collision.

Output: a pure `isDayFullyBooked` predicate, unit-tested, guarding the day-level calendar update.
</objective>

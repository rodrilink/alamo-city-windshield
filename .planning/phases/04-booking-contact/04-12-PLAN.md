  <what-built>
    Three gap-closure changes to the booking and contact flows:
    - **The blocking gap (CONT-06):** the `/book` booking form now runs Zod validation
      client-side before submitting. Previously it dispatched straight to the server on
      every click, so its inline error messages never rendered for any input.
    - **WR-02:** after someone else takes your slot mid-booking, the taken time is now
      deselected so you are returned to the time grid — but your typed name, last name,
      phone and VIN are carried over so you never re-type them.
    - **WR-01:** losing a race on one time slot no longer marks the entire day as fully
      booked in the month calendar. The day is only greyed out when every slot is genuinely taken.
    - Maximum-length caps were also added to both forms' validation schemas (server-side
      hardening; no visible change for normal input).
  </what-built>

  <what-built>
    The complete booking and contact flow:
    - `/book` — a month calendar with fully-booked, past, out-of-window and Sunday dates disabled; a per-date slot list where booked and past slots are identically disabled; a booking form capturing name, phone and optional VIN; and a confirmation screen.
    - `/contact` — the contact form (first name, last name, phone required; address optional) and a VIN search reusing the home page's decoder, whose estimate CTA now navigates to `/book`.
    - Both writes go through Server Actions to the live Supabase database, with a honeypot, server-side Zod re-validation, and server-side slot-legality re-validation in front of every insert.
    - Double-booking is prevented by the database's `UNIQUE (appt_date, appt_time)` constraint, caught as Postgres error `23505`.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Work through all five ROADMAP success criteria in order. Report the criterion number and pass/fail for each.

    **Criterion 1 — calendar and slot display (BOOK-01, BOOK-02, BOOK-03)**
    1. Visit http://localhost:3000/book
    2. Confirm a month calendar renders with working previous/next month navigation and visible chevron icons (not broken placeholders).
    3. Confirm Sundays are not selectable, dates before today are not selectable, and dates more than 30 days out are not selectable.
    4. Confirm **today IS selectable** — same-day booking is allowed by D-04.
    5. Select a weekday and confirm a slot list appears.
    6. Select a **Saturday** and confirm it offers exactly three slots: 9:00, 10:30 and 12:00. There must be **no 1:30 slot** — it would run past the 2:00 PM close.
    7. If you select today, confirm any slot whose start time has already passed appears disabled rather than hidden.

    **Criterion 2 — booking, confirmation, and double-booking (BOOK-06, BOOK-07, BOOK-05)**
    8. Pick an open date and slot, fill in name, last name and phone, and submit.
    9. Confirm a confirmation screen appears showing the date, the time, your name, the shop phone number, and an explicit line saying you will receive a phone call to confirm.
    10. Confirm the booking actually landed: check the `bookings` table in the Supabase dashboard and verify the row exists with `status` = `pending`.
    11. **The critical test.** Open `/book` in a second tab, select the *same date and slot you just booked*, fill the form, and submit.
    12. Confirm you get a "that slot was just taken" style message — **not** a generic error, and not a silent failure.
    13. Confirm your entered name and phone are **still filled in** after that failure (you should only need to pick a different time).
    14. Confirm the taken slot now renders as disabled without you reloading the page.
    15. Confirm the `bookings` table still has exactly **one** row for that date/time — the duplicate was rejected by the database, not merely hidden by the UI.

    **Criterion 3 — contact form (CONT-01, CONT-04, CONT-05)**
    16. Visit http://localhost:3000/contact
    17. Submit with first name, last name and phone filled and address left empty. Confirm a success confirmation message appears.
    18. Confirm the row landed in the `contacts` table in the Supabase dashboard.

    **Criterion 4 — contact page VIN search (CONT-02)**
    19. On `/contact`, enter a valid 17-character VIN (any real VIN, e.g. from your own vehicle) in the VIN search field.
    20. Confirm it returns the same vehicle identity and price range the home page produces for that same VIN.

    22. Confirm the booking form on `/book` shows the vehicle, and that the VIN field is pre-filled.

    **Criterion 5 — form validation (CONT-06)**
    23. On `/contact`, clear all fields and press submit. Confirm inline validation errors appear on the required fields and that nothing is submitted.
    24. On `/book`, do the same on the booking form. Confirm the same behavior.

    27. Confirm neither page snap-scrolls, and that the top navigation behaves as it does on `/about` (normal flow, not an overlay).

    **Brand check**

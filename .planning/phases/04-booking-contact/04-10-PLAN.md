<constraints>
## D-09 MUST SURVIVE — this is confirmed-passing human-UAT behavior

Human UAT step 13 confirmed: after a real duplicate booking was rejected, the entered name
and phone were still filled in. That behavior is protected and must not regress.

The current D-09 mechanism is `useForm({ values: state.values })` inside `BookingForm` —
every non-success `BookingActionState` return repopulates the form from the values the
Server Action echoes back. **`BookingForm`'s `useActionState` state dies if the component
unmounts.** Since WR-02's fix clears `selectedTime` and `SlotList` renders
`{selectedTime && <BookingForm .../>}`, a naive `setSelectedTime(null)` would unmount
`BookingForm` and destroy exactly the values UAT step 13 verified. This plan therefore
lifts the preserved values into `SlotList` before clearing the selection. Read Task 2
carefully — this tension is the whole point of the task.

## Other constraints

- Do NOT change `createBooking` in `src/lib/booking/booking-actions.ts`. Its contract
  (honeypot → Zod → slot-legality → insert, `23505`-only branching, `values` preserved on
  every non-success path) is verified correct and out of scope.
- Do NOT add a pre-check `SELECT` before insert anywhere. D-08's TOCTOU-avoidance is
  deliberate; the `UNIQUE` constraint is the sole arbiter.
- Do NOT upgrade `@hookform/resolvers` — it is pinned to v3 (3.10.0) by explicit human approval.
- Keep the hidden `serverVehicleDesc` field's D-19 guarantee: `vehicleDesc` is derived
  server-side and must continue to reach `createBooking` via form data, never from
  `BookingFormValues` (the type has no field for it, by design).
- Keep the honeypot visually-hidden and NOT `type="hidden"` (D-14).
- `state.status === 'error'` must still NOT disable the slot (D-10) — only `'slot-taken'` does.
</constraints>

<!-- Verified during planning. The formula below was executed against all six D-06 rows and all six passed. -->

**The formula, exactly (D-01 through D-05):**
- `subtotal = 300 + sizeModifier + glassModifier`
- `sizeModifier` is `0` for `car`, `75` for `suv-truck`, `150` for `van-oversized`
- `glassModifier` is `0` for `standard`, `100` for `acoustic`, `200` for `heated`
- `low = Math.round(subtotal * 0.90)`
- `high = Math.round(subtotal * 1.10) + (modelYear >= 2018 ? 250 : 0)`

**Rounding is `Math.round` — half-up.** `Math.round` is applied to the ±10% band *before* the ADAS term is added, never after. Nearest-dollar, not nearest-$5: nearest-$5 would produce 335/340 and 660/665, which contradict the locked D-06 table.

**The six locked D-06 fixtures (all verified to reconcile with the formula above):**

| # | Vehicle | modelYear | sizeBucket | glassType | subtotal | expected low | expected high |
|---|---------|-----------|------------|-----------|----------|--------------|---------------|
| 1 | 2015 Honda Civic (sedan) | 2015 | `car` | `standard` | 300 | 270 | 330 |
| 2 | 2020 Toyota Camry (sedan) | 2020 | `car` | `standard` | 300 | 270 | 580 |
| 3 | 2020 Toyota Camry (sedan) | 2020 | `car` | `acoustic` | 400 | 360 | 690 |
| 4 | 2022 Ford F-150 (pickup) | 2022 | `suv-truck` | `standard` | 375 | 338 | 663 |
| 5 | 2023 Honda Odyssey (van) | 2023 | `van-oversized` | `heated` | 650 | 585 | 965 |
| 6 | 2016 Chevy Silverado (pickup) | 2016 | `suv-truck` | `standard` | 375 | 338 | 413 |

**Row 5 is $585 – $965, NOT $585 – $995.** The `$995` in earlier drafts was a 6↔9 transposition typo. The user was asked directly and confirmed the D-05 formula is authoritative. Do NOT introduce any adjustment term to try to reach $995 — there is no such term.

<objective>
Fix the three CRITICAL defects confirmed by `06-REVIEW.md`. All three are latent — invisible at current data volume and on a Chicago-set dev machine — and surface silently on Vercel (UTC) as wrong numbers with no error.

- **CR-01** — `supabase/config.toml:18` sets `max_rows = 1000`. PostgREST enforces it server-side and returns **HTTP 200 with a truncated body**, so `.error` is null and the existing guard never fires. `getSummaryTotals` has no time filter and no limit, so past 1000 lifetime `page_view` rows (~200 sessions) the Visitors card freezes permanently. `getVisitorSeries` has no `.order()`, so its truncated subset is nondeterministic and whole days vanish at random.
- **CR-02** — three day definitions on one data path: `getVisitorSeries` dedupes by `createdAt.slice(0, 10)` (UTC), `bucketByDay` buckets by `format(new Date(ts), 'yyyy-MM-dd')` (host-local), and the business day is America/Chicago. A session spanning UTC midnight gets two dedupe keys — reintroducing the double-count 06-06 fixed.
- **CR-03** — `getServerNow()` feeds Chicago wall-clock parts into `new Date(y, m-1, d, h, min)`, which interprets them in the **host** zone: a 5-hour skew on Vercel. Its own docstring claims to avoid this.

`src/lib/server-time.ts` is the project's existing timezone authority — it already exports `BUSINESS_TIME_ZONE` ('America/Chicago'), `getBusinessNowParts()`, `getBusinessTodayDateString()` and `formatLocalDateKey()`. This plan REUSES it. Do not introduce a fourth day definition.
</objective>
